# DeFaria NYC: Go-to-Market Plan

## Overview

Turn DeFaria NYC from a production pipeline with zero paying clients into a real digital marketing, distribution & seeding agency generating $25K-$75K/mo from 5-15 label/artist campaigns within 6 months.

This plan covers everything between "today" and "first paying client" and beyond — including the business infrastructure that's missing: campaign scoping, contracts, onboarding, reporting, and the sales machine.

## Problem Statement

DeFaria has strong production infrastructure (FFmpeg + WhisperX + Postiz pipeline, 7 active seeding accounts, website, pricing deck, 78+ contact outreach list) but **zero paying clients** under the new model. The gap isn't capability — it's business operations:

- No defined campaign deliverables (what does $5K actually buy?)
- No client contract or service agreement
- No onboarding flow
- No performance reporting template
- No case study with real numbers
- Old monthly pricing model conflicts with new campaign-based model
- "500M+ views" claim on website is unsubstantiated

## Proposed Solution

Execute a 3-phase go-to-market over 6 months, building business operations alongside client acquisition:

1. **Phase 1 (March-April):** Build proof — case studies, contracts, reporting. Run discounted campaigns for Nolan's artists.
2. **Phase 2 (May-June):** First real clients — use proof to land 2-3 campaigns through artist-up sales.
3. **Phase 3 (July-Sept):** Scale — hire editors, grow account network, target $25K+/mo.

### Payment Structure

| Payment Event | Timing |
|---|---|
| **Deposit** | 50% at contract signing (non-refundable) |
| **Final payment** | 50% at campaign midpoint (week 2 for Spark, week 2-3 for Push/Flood) |
| **Custom campaigns** | 3 installments: 40% deposit / 30% midpoint / 30% completion |

**Rules:**
- No Net 30 for campaigns under $15K. Period.
- Custom campaigns ($15K+) can negotiate Net 15 on the final 30%.
- Late payment = campaign pauses after 48-hour grace period.
- Accepted methods: Wire, Zelle, Stripe (client pays 3% processing fee for Stripe).

### Client Service Agreement (Key Terms)

Create a 2-3 page service agreement template covering:

**Scope:** Campaign tier, deliverables, timeline, platforms.

**Content Ownership:**
- DeFaria retains right to use clips in portfolio/case studies (anonymized if requested).
- Client owns the content created for their campaign.
- Client provides or authorizes all source material — DeFaria not liable for copyright claims on client-provided content.

**Approval Process:**
- Client approves clips in batches of 5-7 via WhatsApp or email.
- 24-hour approval window. Silence after 24 hours = approval granted.
- If client consistently delays approvals (3+ missed windows), campaign timeline extends at no additional cost up to 1 week, then $500/week overage.

**Performance:**
- NO view count guarantees. Explicitly stated.
- DeFaria guarantees deliverable volume (X clips, Y platforms, Z seeding accounts).
- If DeFaria underdelivers on volume, bonus clips are added to compensate.

**Cancellation:**
- Campaign can be paused once for up to 14 days (release delays are common).
- Cancellation after deposit: deposit is non-refundable. Any work completed is delivered.
- Cancellation after midpoint payment: no refund, campaign delivers remaining work.

**Confidentiality:**
- DeFaria does not disclose client campaigns to competitors.
- Client does not disclose DeFaria's pricing to other clients.

**File:** `docs/templates/client-service-agreement.md` (draft, then get lawyer to review before first client)

### Client Onboarding Checklist

When a client signs, collect within 48 hours:

**From the client:**
- [ ] Source content (raw footage, music files, photos) via Google Drive or WeTransfer
- [ ] Brand guidelines (if any — logos, fonts, colors, do/don't list)
- [ ] Approval contact name + WhatsApp/phone + email
- [ ] Release date and timeline
- [ ] Key messaging (what angle do they want? What should NOT be said?)
- [ ] Platform credentials (if posting to their accounts) OR confirmation DeFaria posts from owned accounts

**DeFaria sets up:**
- [ ] Dedicated editor assigned (Push and above)
- [ ] Seeding account pool allocated (no cross-client contamination)
- [ ] Campaign calendar created (clip schedule for full duration)
- [ ] First batch of 5-7 clips produced and sent for approval
- [ ] Reporting sheet initialized

**Target:** First clip live within 5 business days of signed contract + deposit.

### Content Approval Workflow

```
DeFaria produces batch of 5-7 clips
        ↓
Sends to client via WhatsApp (video files + captions)
        ↓
Client has 24 hours to respond
        ↓
    ┌─── Approved → clips go live on schedule
    ├─── Feedback → 1 revision round, resubmit within 24hr
    └─── No response → auto-approved after 24hr (per contract)
```

**For Spark:** Sean sends directly to artist.
**For Push/Flood:** Editor produces, Sean reviews, then sends to client.
**For Custom:** Editor produces, Sean reviews, account manager sends to client.

### Phase 3: Agency Mode (July - September 2026)

**Goal:** Scale to 5+ active campaigns, hire editors, build the network.

#### Hiring

- **Editor #2:** Hire when campaign volume exceeds what Sean + Editor #1 can handle (likely at 3 simultaneous campaigns).
- **Compensation:** $1,000-$1,500/mo base + $50/clip bonus for clips over 50K views. Contractor, not employee.
- **Editor #3:** Hire when running 5+ simultaneous campaigns.
- **Quality control:** Sean reviews every clip before it goes to client for the first 2 weeks. After that, spot-check 20% of clips.

#### Account Network

- Target: 50+ seeding accounts across TikTok, IG, X
- **Account creation pipeline:** Web signup, Yahoo emails, different browsers per account, 24hr wait before connecting to Postiz (per docs/solutions learnings)
- **Cross-client isolation:** Dedicated account pools per client. No cross-pollination. Shared reserve pool for surge campaigns only.
- **Ban recovery:** Keep 20% surplus accounts as backups. When an account gets banned, swap in backup within 24 hours.

#### Operations

- **Account management tool:** Simple spreadsheet tracking: account handle, platform, client assigned, status (active/banned/backup), follower count, last post date.
- **Editor management:** Weekly 15-min check-in per editor. Clip style spec (already locked in CLAUDE.md) is the quality standard.
- **Client communication:** All through WhatsApp groups (1 group per client: Sean + editor + client approval contact).

**Revenue target:** $25K+/mo from 5+ simultaneous campaigns.

## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Campaigns underperform (avg 2K views) | High | High | Set expectations at onboarding: "We guarantee volume, not views." Include in contract. Over-deliver on clip count. |
| Labels demand Net 30-60 payment | Medium | High | Firm: 50% deposit upfront, no exceptions under $15K. Custom campaigns can negotiate final installment only. |
| Seeding accounts get banned | High | Medium | 20% surplus accounts as backups. Documented recovery process. Rotate posting patterns. |
| Nolan relationship gets complicated | Medium | High | Put Riddle LLC pricing in writing NOW. Separate vendor invoices. No ambiguity. |
| Scope creep on campaigns | High | Medium | Service agreement defines exact deliverables. Overages billed at $500/week. |
| Editor quality inconsistent | Medium | Medium | Clip style spec is locked. Sean reviews 100% initially, then 20% spot-check. |
| Cold outreach gets no responses | Medium | Medium | Artist-up path is primary. Cold outreach is supplemental. Don't depend on it. |
