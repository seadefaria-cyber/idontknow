## Why This Approach

### The Core Insight
KHAITE has intentionally gone silent on social media. They're not failing at social. They're choosing silence as a luxury signal. But 12.2K TikTok posts are happening without them. The Kendall campaign was designed for virality (bleached brows, bag-as-top) but has no distribution strategy beyond editorial.

### The Pitch in One Line
"KHAITE stays silent. We make the internet unable to stop talking about you."

### Why DeFaria Over Anyone Else
- Nobody is doing AI-powered content distribution specifically for luxury fashion. Maison Meta does AI creative. Clipping Agency does distribution. Devotion does AI creator management. Nobody combines all three for luxury. That's the white space.
- Speed. Agencies take weeks to produce content briefs. DeFaria produces content the same day. AI handles production, humans handle taste.
- Cultural proximity. Sean is in his 20s, in Brooklyn, embedded in the exact world KHAITE's customer romanticizes. "You get me, not an account manager at a 200-person agency."
- Operational efficiency. One person with AI infrastructure moves faster than a 40-person agency with overhead.

### What NOT To Do In The Pitch
- Do NOT lead with AI or technology. Frame as cultural strategy, not tech.
- Do NOT mention bots, automation, or scale. Luxury brands are running AWAY from AI in creative.
- Do NOT cite vanity metrics like "500M views." Luxury cares about WHO saw it, not how many.
- Do NOT pitch volume ("200-500 content pieces"). KHAITE is built on scarcity and restraint.
- Do NOT encourage UGC/duets/stitches. KHAITE fears brand dilution more than anything.
- Do NOT make it feel like a generic pitch with KHAITE's name swapped in. Reference Drew Vickers, the bleached brows, the bag-as-top, the 1980s NYC noir aesthetic.

## The Three Strategies

### Strategy 1: Invisible Amplification Layer
KHAITE doesn't post. They don't need to. We become the infrastructure that ensures the Kendall campaign imagery saturates fashion TikTok, Instagram, and Reddit through curated placement on real fashion and culture accounts that match KHAITE's editorial standard. The brand's hands never touch social media. We're the invisible hand.

**Why it works:** Exactly what Bottega does with @newbottega and fan accounts, but engineered instead of left to chance. KHAITE maintains silence. The internet does the talking.

**How it works operationally:**
- Build a network of 50-80 fashion/culture accounts across three tiers (Tier 1: 100K-500K, Tier 2: 25K-100K, Tier 3: 5K-25K)
- Distribute campaign assets with suggested captions, adapted for each account's voice
- Stagger placements to create the illusion of organic discovery
- Pay Tier 2/3 accounts ($50-200/post) in early months while building organic relationships
- Monitor and track every placement for reach, engagement, and sentiment

### Strategy 2: Cultural Moment Engineering
Instead of always-on content, identify 8-10 strategic moments per year where KHAITE should own the conversation. Pre-build content packages for each moment. Deploy within hours, not weeks. When Kendall trends, KHAITE trends.

**Key moments for KHAITE SS2026:**
1. NYFW (Sep 2026) -- KHAITE's own show
2. Met Gala (May 2026) -- If Kendall attends
3. Kendall paparazzi moments -- Any time she's photographed in KHAITE
4. Awards season red carpets (Jan-Mar)
5. Major editorial drops (Vogue, BoF, WWD features)
6. Viral fashion moments (when #quietluxury trends, bag-as-clothing goes viral)

**Why it works:** Loewe turned a tomato meme into a real bag in days. We're proposing that same speed, systematized.

**Critical requirement:** Same-day content approval from KHAITE. Without fast approval, this strategy is dead.

### Strategy 3: The "Next Katie Holmes Moment" Machine
Engineer one surgical, shareable hero moment around the bag-as-top concept. Coordinate its appearance across fashion editorial, creator accounts, and culture pages simultaneously. One moment. Maximum impact.

**The playbook (proven by case studies):**
- Jacquemus: One CGI bags video, 10 seconds = 40-70M views, 900% search spike
- Miu Miu: One micro-mini skirt coordinated across 4 magazine covers = revenue from $329M to $648M
- Loewe: Responded to a meme with a real product in days = 900K views in 5 hours

**The play:**
- Create 3-5 hero content pieces around the bag-as-top (video, carousel, reaction format, editorial take)
- Coordinate 10-15 accounts to post within the same 24-48 hour window
- Seed the CONVERSATION, not just the image ("Is the bag-as-top genius or insane?")
- Design content to trigger organic reactions and press pickup

**Why it works:** Every viral luxury moment was ONE thing, done perfectly, at the right time. Not 500 pieces. One surgical strike.

### Supporting: Intelligence Layer
AI-powered social listening monitoring #khaite conversations, tracking sentiment, measuring share of voice vs The Row, Bottega Veneta, Loewe, and Peter Do. Weekly reports on quality of conversation (what adjectives people use about KHAITE), not vanity metrics.

## Key Decisions

1. **No pricing in the deck.** Let them see the vision first. Money conversation happens after they're bought in. With their budget, we don't want to anchor low.
2. **Lead with taste, not technology.** AI is operational speed, not creative decision-making. "Every creative decision is human. The tools just let me move faster."
3. **Position Sean as cultural proximity, not agency scale.** "You get me, not an account manager."
4. **Propose a contained pilot, not a full campaign.** Remove the risk for KHAITE.
5. **Measure in luxury terms.** Share of voice, sentiment quality, earned media value. Not views.
6. **The bag-as-top is our hero asset.** It's the most memeable, shareable, conversation-starting element of the campaign.
7. **Reference specific KHAITE details.** Drew Vickers, bleached brows, 1980s NYC noir, Catherine Holstein's vision. Show we see the brand.
