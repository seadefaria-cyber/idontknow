# DeFaria NYC — Digital Marketing, Distribution & Seeding Business Plan

**Date:** 2026-03-04
**Status:** Brainstorm
**Author:** Sean DeFaria + Claude

## What We're Building

**DeFaria NYC** is a digital marketing, distribution, and seeding agency for music releases. Not a clip shop. Not an account farm. A full-service partner that labels and managers hire when they need a release to reach every corner of the internet.

**"Distribution" in our context = digital distribution of content and attention.** We distribute clips, visuals, and promotional content across social platforms, fan pages, influencer networks, and repost accounts. We are NOT a DSP distributor (that's DistroKid/TuneCore). We distribute ATTENTION.

**Target scale:** 5-15 active clients, 3-5 editors, $25K-$75K/mo revenue.

**Pricing model:** Campaign-based ($3K-$10K+ per release cycle), not monthly retainers.

---

## The Identity Problem (And How We Fix It)

### What DeFaria Is NOT

- Not "the clip guy"
- Not "the account farm"
- Not "the seeding network"
- Not a social media manager posting daily content

Clipping and seeding accounts are **tools** — not the identity. Calling yourself a "clipping agency" is like a marketing firm calling itself "the email sender."

### What DeFaria IS

A **digital marketing and distribution agency for music.** When a label drops a single, album, or video — DeFaria makes sure it's everywhere. We handle the entire digital push:

- **Content creation** — clips, visuals, title cards
- **Digital distribution** — seeding across fan pages, repost networks, influencer accounts
- **Platform strategy** — TikTok, IG, X, YouTube Shorts, optimized per-platform
- **Campaign management** — timing, rollout strategy, data tracking, reporting

The seeding network is the engine. The marketing strategy is the product.

## Why DeFaria Wins

### The Wedge: Speed + Underground Access

LV8 is a Los Angeles "Integrated Social-First Agency" that runs campaigns across entertainment, lifestyle, sports, and tech. They're generalists. When a label hires them for an underground rap artist, they're hiring suits to study the culture.

**DeFaria IS the culture.** We're already:
- Running active accounts in the underground rap ecosystem
- Posting daily, tracking what works, learning the algorithm in real-time
- Embedded in the community — not researching it from outside
- Producing content at near-zero cost (all tools run locally)

### The Pitch

> "We don't send you a deck. We show you what we're already doing."

When DeFaria walks into a meeting (or sends an email), the pitch isn't a PowerPoint — it's live accounts with real posts, real engagement, real followers. The proof IS the pitch.

### Speed to Market

A label says "we're dropping Friday." LV8 needs a week to brief their team, source creators, build a plan. DeFaria can have clips seeded across 20+ accounts by Thursday night. In music, timing is everything.

### Cost Structure Advantage

DeFaria's production costs are near-zero:
- Clips: $0/unit (FFmpeg + WhisperX, local)
- Captions: $0 (ASS subtitle rendering, local)
- Posting: $39-99/mo (Postiz)
- Accounts: Already built, already growing

A $5K campaign is almost pure profit. For LV8, that same campaign has creator fees, paid media spend, team overhead, and office rent.

## Campaign Pricing

| Tier | Price | Duration | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Spark** | $3,000 | 4 weeks | Content creation + distribution across core seeding network. Weekly reporting. |
| **Push** | $5,000 | 6 weeks | Expanded network + higher volume. Strategy call. Weekly reporting. |
| **Flood** | $10,000 | 8 weeks | Full network activation. Dedicated editor. Daily reporting. Influencer seeding. |
| **Custom** | $15K+ | Flexible | Everything above + editorial placement, comment warfare, multi-platform blitz. |

**Why campaign-based (not monthly):**
- Labels budget per release, not per month
- Easier to justify a one-time $5K spend than $5K/mo ongoing
- Natural upsell: "Single did well — want us to run the album rollout?"
- Matches how PR firms, playlist promoters, and radio promoters already bill

## Sales Path: Artist-Up

### The Flywheel

```
1. Deliver results for an artist (Nettspend = proof of concept)
2. Artist tells manager: "DeFaria is getting me numbers"
3. Manager requests seeding budget from label for next release
4. Label allocates digital marketing budget → DeFaria gets the campaign
5. Campaign succeeds → label gives you the next artist on their roster
6. Repeat. Compound. Scale.
```

### Why This Works Better Than Cold Email

- **Trust is pre-built.** The artist already vouches for you.
- **No cold pitch needed.** Label marketing hears about you from their own artist.
- **Budget flows naturally.** Managers request budget from labels all the time.
- **Retention is organic.** Good results = next release = next campaign.

### Immediate Path

1. **Nettspend → case study.** Compile real numbers (views, growth, engagement) into a proof deck.
2. **Nettspend's manager → warm intro** to other artists on the label roster.
3. **Nolan's 3 clients → first paying campaigns.** Riddle LLC (Nolan's management company) is a vendor client of DeFaria. His artists are signed to labels. Each one is a door.
4. **One discounted proof campaign** for a new artist. Not free — discounted. Show what 4 weeks of full digital distribution looks like. Then convert to full price.

## Relationship Structure: DeFaria vs. Riddle LLC

**DeFaria NYC** (Sean) = Digital marketing, distribution, and seeding agency.
**Riddle LLC** (Nolan) = Artist management company.

These are **separate businesses**. Riddle LLC is a client of DeFaria, not a partner. Nolan's artists are natural first customers, but DeFaria runs campaigns independently and bills Riddle LLC (or the label directly) as a vendor.

This keeps the businesses clean and lets DeFaria take clients from anywhere — not just Nolan's roster.
