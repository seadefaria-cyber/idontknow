## What We're Building

A solo, AI-powered digital marketing agency focused on music artists and labels. DeFaria LLC helps artists roll out releases and stay relevant online by combining proprietary AI content tools with creative campaign strategy.

**Core offering:** Full-service campaign execution for album/single releases — from strategy to content creation to distribution — powered by an AI pipeline that produces 10x the content at a fraction of the cost of traditional agencies.

**Target clients:** Underground to mid-tier artists (up to ~1M monthly Spotify listeners) with label deals or management, meaning they have real marketing budgets.

**Revenue target:** $10K/month personal income within 3-6 months.

## Why This Approach

### Phase 1: Proof Campaigns (Month 1-2 — March/April 2026)

**Goal:** Build undeniable case studies by running 1-2 campaigns for free or heavily discounted.

**How it works:**
- Pick 1-2 artists from your network who have upcoming releases
- Run a full campaign using the viral clipper pipeline + creative strategy
- Document everything: clip volume, view counts, engagement rates, follower growth, streaming bumps
- Package results into a clean case study (numbers, visuals, timeline)

**What you deliver per campaign:**
1. **Campaign strategy** — Rollout timeline, content calendar, platform priorities
2. **Short-form content** — 20-30+ viral clips generated from their content via AI pipeline
3. **Multi-platform distribution** — Posted across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Twitter
4. **Guerrilla/viral tactics** — Meme seeding, narrative angles, fan engagement plays (like the Nettspend campaign concepts)
5. **Performance report** — Full analytics breakdown at campaign end

**Why free/discounted:**
- Eliminates risk for the artist → easy yes
- You refine your workflow before money is on the line
- The case study becomes your most powerful sales asset

### Phase 2: Premium Positioning (Month 3-6 — May-August 2026)

**Goal:** Use proof campaigns to land paying mid-tier clients at $5-8K per campaign.

**How it works:**
- Take case studies to every manager connection you have
- Cold outreach to mid-tier artist managers with the pitch: "I did X for [artist], here are the numbers, here's what I'll do for yours"
- Charge $5-8K per campaign — you only need 2 campaigns/month to hit $10K
- Position the AI pipeline as your differentiator — "proprietary tech that creates 10x the content at a fraction of the cost"

**The pitch (to managers):**
> "I run a small agency with proprietary AI tools that generate 30+ unique clips from a single video, with captions, hooks, and platform-optimized formatting — automatically. I pair that with creative campaign strategy. I just did [X results] for [artist]. I'd love to do the same for [your artist] around their next release."

## Workflow (AI-Maximized)

### Per-Campaign Workflow

1. **Intake** — Client sends raw content (long-form videos, music, assets)
2. **Strategy** — You + Claude design the campaign arc, content calendar, angles
3. **Content Generation** — Viral clipper pipeline processes videos → 30+ clips with captions, hooks
4. **Review & Approve** — You review clips, pick the best, make adjustments
5. **Distribution** — Automated posting across platforms on schedule
6. **Monitor & Adapt** — Track performance, double down on what's working
7. **Report** — End-of-campaign analytics delivered to client

**Your time per campaign: ~10-15 hours** (mostly strategy, review, and client communication)
**AI handles:** Clip generation, captioning, hook writing, scheduling, posting, analytics aggregation

## Client Acquisition Playbook

### Month 1-2: Proof Phase
- Reach out to manager contacts — offer free campaign for their next release
- Pick artists with upcoming drops (timing matters)
- Document EVERYTHING — screen record analytics, save all content, track metrics daily

### Month 3+: Paid Phase
- **Warm outreach:** Send case studies to every manager/industry contact
- **Cold outreach:** DM managers on Instagram/Twitter with the case study + short pitch
- **Content marketing:** Post your own clips showing before/after campaign results
- **Referrals:** Ask proof-campaign artists to introduce you to other managers
- **Industry events:** NYC has constant music industry events — show up, network, follow up

### The Sales Funnel
1. Case study lands in manager's inbox/DMs
2. They see the numbers and respond
3. 15-min call: learn about their artist, upcoming release, goals
4. You send a 1-page proposal (scope, timeline, price)
5. They say yes → invoice → start
