## What AI Actually Does vs What Humans Do

### AI handles (Layer 2: Production & Distribution)
- Text overlays, captions (WhisperX transcription + FFmpeg rendering)
- Sound placement on clips
- Formatting for each platform (aspect ratios, durations)
- Scheduling and posting across TikTok/IG/X/YT Shorts
- Performance tracking and weekly report generation
- Clip log maintenance

### Humans handle (Layer 1: Creative Decisions)
- Which moments to clip (the hook — most important decision)
- Caption writing (the open loop that stops the scroll)
- Sound selection (matching energy to footage)
- Posting strategy (what to post when, what's trending)
- Client relationship and communication
- Quality control — every clip gets human eyes before posting

**Rule: AI never touches creative decisions. AI executes what humans decide.**

## Key Metrics to Track

### Per account
- Follower growth (weekly)
- Average views per post (7-day rolling)
- Top-performing post of the week (views + shares)
- Share rate (shares are the #1 algorithm signal)

### Per client
- Total views generated (monthly)
- Follower growth across all accounts (monthly)
- Cost per view (your costs / total views)
- Client satisfaction (are they happy, will they renew?)

### Business
- Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
- Client retention rate
- Average revenue per client
- Hours worked per client per day

## Rules for Yourself

1. **Don't sell what you can't deliver.** Only take on clients you have capacity for.
2. **Quality over quantity.** 1 great clip > 5 mid clips. Your Nettspend data proves this.
3. **Never reveal AI involvement.** Position everything as human creative team.
4. **Weekly reports are non-negotiable.** Every client gets data every Monday.
5. **3-month minimum protects you.** Organic growth takes time. Don't let clients bail after 2 weeks.
6. **Document everything.** Every win becomes a case study. Every loss becomes a lesson.
7. **Editor is the bottleneck, not AI.** Scale by adding editors, not by adding AI volume.
