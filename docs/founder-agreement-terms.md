# Founder Agreement — Key Terms for Lawyer

**Date:** March 3, 2026
**Parties:** Sean DeFaria and Nolan Riddle

---

## The Deal

1. **New LLC** created together — just for the SaaS product (separate from DeFaria LLC and Riddle LLC)
2. **Sean owns 60%, Nolan owns 40%**
3. **4-year vesting** with 1-year cliff — both earn equity over time
4. **Split lawyer cost 50/50** — one neutral lawyer for both sides

---

## Detailed Terms

### Company Structure
- **DeFaria Technologies LLC** — new company for the SaaS product
- DeFaria LLC stays 100% Sean's — unrelated business
- Riddle LLC stays 100% Nolan's — unrelated business
- The new LLC is the only thing they share

### Ownership
- Sean DeFaria: **60%**
- Nolan Riddle: **40%**

### Why 60/40
- Sean built the entire prototype (code, design, integrations)
- Sean funded all costs to date (hosting, tools, infrastructure)
- Sean handles two roles: technology + operations
- Nolan handles one role: sales + relationships
- Both are essential — neither works without the other

### Vesting (Equity Earned Over Time)
- **4-year vesting** for both founders
- **1-year cliff**: if either person leaves before 12 months, they get 0%
- After the cliff, equity vests monthly over the remaining 3 years
- Example: if Nolan leaves after 2 years, he keeps ~17.5% (half of his 40%)
- Example: if Sean leaves after 2 years, he keeps ~32.5% (half of his 60%)

### Roles
- **Sean:** Builds the product, deploys agency portals, manages tech operations, handles support, maintains infrastructure
- **Nolan:** Brings in agency clients, manages relationships, handles sales and business development, industry expertise

### Intellectual Property
- All code, designs, and product IP belong to the new LLC (not to either individual)
- Sean's prior work (the prototype) is assigned to the new LLC
- Sean retains rights to unrelated work under DeFaria LLC

### Client Ownership
- All clients belong to the company, not to either founder individually
- If either founder leaves, clients stay with the company

### Non-Compete
- Neither founder will build, fund, or work on a competing product for 12 months after leaving

### Decision Making
- Major decisions require both founders to agree (selling the company, taking investment, spending over a set amount)
- Day-to-day tech decisions: Sean
- Day-to-day sales decisions: Nolan

### Nolan's Agency as First Client
- Riddle LLC (Nolan's agency) is the first client on the platform
- Nolan pays only infrastructure costs (~$50/month) — not standard pricing
- He's a co-founder, not a regular customer
- All other agencies pay full standard pricing ($500-$1,000+/month)

### Separation of Entities
- Nolan Riddle has NO ownership, interest, or claim in DeFaria LLC or any of Sean DeFaria's other business entities
- Sean DeFaria has NO ownership, interest, or claim in Riddle LLC or any of Nolan Riddle's other business entities
- Nolan's 40% ownership is limited to DeFaria Technologies LLC only

### Dispute Resolution
- Step 1: Founders discuss and try to resolve (30 days)
- Step 2: Mediation with a neutral third party
- Step 3: Binding arbitration (final decision)
- Avoids expensive court battles
- Sean has tiebreaker vote as majority (60%) owner if deadlock persists

### Capital Contributions
- Neither founder is required to invest additional money
- If the company needs cash, both contribute proportionally (60/40) or agree on alternative
- If one founder contributes extra, it's treated as a loan — company pays them back first from revenue

### Profit Distribution
- Profits (after expenses) split 60/40
- Distributed quarterly
- Both founders must agree to reinvest profits instead of distributing

### Buyout Valuation
- If a founder leaves, remaining founder has first right to buy vested shares
- Company valued at 3x annual revenue (or independent appraiser if both prefer)
- Buyout can be paid over 12-24 months (not required as lump sum)

### Death or Disability
- If either founder dies or becomes permanently disabled, the other gets first right to buy their shares at the agreed valuation
- Optional: life insurance policy to fund buyout

### Termination
- Either founder can leave with 60 days written notice
- Departing founder keeps their vested equity only
- Unvested equity is forfeited
- Remaining founder gets first right to buy the departing founder's vested shares

### DeFaria LLC Services Agreement (Separate Contract)
- Riddle LLC exclusively uses DeFaria LLC for digital distribution and marketing services
- Standard rates, 12-month minimum commitment
- This is a separate agreement between Sean's existing company and Nolan's agency
- Has nothing to do with DeFaria Technologies LLC
